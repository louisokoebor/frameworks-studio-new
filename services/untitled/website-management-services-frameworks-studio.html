FAQ

Common
‍Questions.

Q: What does website management include?

A: It depends on your plan, but at a minimum it covers regular content updates, bug fixes, technical maintenance, CMS support, and monthly reporting. Higher-tier plans add SEO improvements, performance optimisation, conversion work, and strategic recommendations.

Q: Do you work on websites you didn’t build?

A: Yes. Most of our management clients come to us with existing websites built by someone else. We audit the site, onboard it into our process, and start managing it. If the site needs improvement work before ongoing management makes sense, we’ll tell you upfront.

Q: Do you offer one-off support?

A: We do, but our management plans are where clients get the most value. One-off work is scoped and quoted separately. If you’re not sure whether you need a one-off fix or ongoing support, start with a free audit and we’ll advise.

Q: How quickly can updates be completed?

A: Minor updates are typically completed within 1–2 business days. Larger changes are scoped separately with a clear timeline. Pro plan clients get priority turnaround.

Q: Do you provide reports?

A: Yes. Growth and Pro plan clients receive monthly reports covering what was completed, site health metrics, and recommended next steps. Essential plan clients receive a summary of completed work.

Q: What platforms do you support?

A: We manage websites on Webflow, WordPress, Shopify, and Framer. If your site runs on a different platform, get in touch and we’ll let you know if we can help.

Q: Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?

A: Yes. Plans can be changed at the start of any billing cycle. We’ll always recommend the right level of support for your needs.
